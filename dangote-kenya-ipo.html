Kenya Approves Dangote Refinery IPO Access for Eligible Investors

October 5, 2026 | Business

Kenya's Capital Markets Authority (CMA) has approved a route for eligible Kenyan investors to participate in the initial public offering (IPO) of Dangote Petroleum Refinery & Petrochemicals through Global Depositary Receipts (GDRs).

The approval allows eligible investors in Kenya to access the Nigerian refinery's IPO through Renaissance Capital Kenya, subject to the applicable regulatory requirements.

What the approval means

The development gives eligible Kenyan investors an opportunity to participate in the Dangote Refinery IPO through the GDR structure. The GDRs are expected to provide a route for investors to gain exposure to the Nigerian company through the Nairobi Securities Exchange, subject to the relevant approvals.

The Capital Markets Authority clarified that its approval should not be interpreted as an investment recommendation. Investors are expected to consider the risks and other relevant information before making an investment decision.

Not the proposed Lamu refinery

The approval relates to the Dangote Petroleum Refinery in Nigeria. It should not be confused with the proposed Lamu refinery project in Kenya.

IPO closing date

The Dangote Refinery IPO opened on September 14, 2026, and is scheduled to close on October 13, 2026.

Eligible investors should rely on official regulatory and offer documents for the full terms, conditions and risks associated with the IPO.
